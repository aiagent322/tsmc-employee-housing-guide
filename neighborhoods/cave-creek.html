What Makes Cave Creek Different

Cave Creek sits on the Carefree Highway — the same road Dominion's office is on, the same road that runs west to the Loop 303 and TSMC. The commute is 25–35 minutes. What makes Cave Creek different from the Loop 303 master-planned corridor is character: the town resisted the density that absorbed every community around it. Lot sizes range from quarter-acre Tatum Ranch homes to 5-acre foothills parcels. Old Town Cave Creek on Cave Creek Road has a working cluster of western restaurants, bars, and shops. The 2,922-acre Cave Creek Regional Park and 2,154-acre Spur Cross Ranch Conservation Area are both within 10 minutes of any Cave Creek address — trail systems that outdoor-oriented employees from Taiwan, accustomed to Taiwan's mountain trail networks, find immediately compelling.

Tatum Ranch — The Entry Point

Cave Creek's largest master-planned community — 3,400+ homes across 30 sub-neighborhoods built by 12 different builders from the late 1980s through the early 2000s. Townhomes from the $400Ks, single-family homes $600K–$900K, with gated enclaves at $900K–$1.2M. Tatum Ranch Golf Club (private, 18-hole, Robert Cupp design) is separate from HOA dues. The community has a genuine diversity of architectural styles due to the multi-builder history. CCUSD throughout — Cactus Shadows High School is the feeder.

Cave Creek Foothills — Custom Acreage

North of downtown Cave Creek, the market shifts to 2–5 acre custom lots on private wells and septic, no HOA, Black Mountain views. Custom homes $1M–$3M+. Well and septic due diligence, wildfire insurance availability, and jurisdictional verification (Town of Cave Creek vs. unincorporated Maricopa County) are the required pre-offer steps. Dominion's Carefree Highway office has handled foothills transactions and knows the specific issues.

Schools — CCUSD

Cave Creek Unified School District serves all of 85331. B+ Niche rating, top 30 in Arizona. Cactus Shadows High School is the feeder high school for all Cave Creek and Carefree addresses. Full school comparison →
