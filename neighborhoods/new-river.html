What New River Offers

New River is the rural tier of the TSMC commute corridor — 2 to 5+ acre parcels on private wells and septic, mostly no-HOA, bordering the Tonto National Forest to the north. It is genuinely rural: dark skies, low density, horses and RVs are normal. The lifestyle is quieter than any master-planned community and significantly less constrained. New River is served by DVUSD — Boulder Creek High School among its feeder schools — which gives it the same A-rated school district as Norterra and Anthem at a lower price point and larger lot size.

The Commute

I-17 south is the primary commute route from New River to TSMC. The drive is 30–40 minutes from most New River addresses to the campus entrance at 43rd Avenue and Dove Valley Road. This is 10–20 minutes longer than Norterra but comparable to Cave Creek and Carefree. New River is more rural than Desert Hills and further north — the trade-off is larger lots and more open space at similar or lower prices.

Prices — Mid-2026

Median sale price in New River reached $695K in June 2026, up approximately 7% year-over-year. Active inventory compressed significantly in the 12 months prior as TSMC employee demand extended into the rural corridor. Well and acreage lots $200K–$500K for raw land; improved homes $500K–$1.5M+.

Due Diligence

Same requirements as Desert Hills: private well (verify permit, pump test, water quality) and septic inspection before closing. New River parcels also more frequently border state trust land or Tonto National Forest land — verify exact parcel boundaries and legal access. Well depths and water quality vary significantly within New River; an experienced agent with local transaction history is essential.
