Two Markets Under One Name

Anthem is a Del Webb master-planned community of approximately 6,000 homes in the 85086/85087 zip codes — but it contains two meaningfully different products. Anthem Parkside is the larger non-gated sub-community where most family buyers land. Anthem Country Club is a separate gated golf community with guard-staffed entry, 18 holes, and a clubhouse. The price ranges, lifestyles, and HOA structures are distinct.

Anthem Parkside — The Family Market

The most family-oriented community in the TSMC commute corridor. Anthem Community Park — 63 acres — includes a full community center with indoor and outdoor pools, a water park (Anthem's beloved community splash pad), skate park, tennis, basketball, walking/biking paths, and one of the most active community event calendars in North Phoenix. Single-family homes range $500K–$900K; townhomes and condos start around $360K. The entire community is served by DVUSD — all five DVUSD high schools received ADE "A" grades for 2024–25. Boulder Creek High School serves Anthem and was named an Arizona A+ School of Excellence in 2025.

Anthem Country Club — The Golf & Gated Market

A guard-gated community with an 18-hole golf course and a clubhouse with dining, fitness, and tennis. Homes are predominantly single-story (a design choice common to Del Webb communities that appeals to many Arizona buyers). Prices range $750K–$2M+. The Country Club HOA includes club access; confirm current membership terms and dues structure with the club before purchasing. The gated environment and lower density appeal to senior TSMC managers and executives who want security and resort amenity access without Carefree's longer commute.

Schools

DVUSD throughout — ADE "A" district. Boulder Creek High School (Anthem/New River feeder) received an ADE "A" grade and was named Arizona A+ School of Excellence in 2025. Canyon Springs STEM Academy (K–8, Cognia-certified) is available via open enrollment — particularly relevant for children of TSMC engineers. Full school guide →

Commute & Location

Anthem is approximately 25–35 minutes from TSMC via I-17 south to the Loop 303 west, then north to the campus entrance at 43rd Avenue. The commute is longer than Norterra but the payoff is the most comprehensive family amenity package in the corridor. Anthem is approximately 40 minutes north of downtown Phoenix and 25 minutes from Norterra's retail cluster.
