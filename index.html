The TSMC Arizona Campus

TSMC's North Phoenix campus sits near the northwest corner of 43rd Avenue and the Loop 303 freeway in Phoenix, Arizona 85085 — 1,129 acres of Sonoran Desert that is now the largest foreign direct investment project in American history. Three fabs are in operation or active construction as of 2026. Fab 1 entered high-volume production on N4 process technology in Q4 2024. Fab 2 completed its structure in 2025 with volume production on N3 technology targeted for the second half of 2027. Fab 3 broke ground in April 2025 and will produce N2 and A16 process technologies, targeting production by the end of the decade. Three additional fabs, two advanced packaging facilities, and an R&D center are planned as part of the full $165 billion buildout.

The surrounding master development, called Halo Vista, will eventually add thousands of homes, retail, hotels, and research space in the immediate vicinity. That development is in early stages as of mid-2026. For now, employees commute to the campus from established residential communities along the I-17 and Loop 303 corridors to the south and east.

Key commute fact: The campus entrance is near 43rd Avenue and Dove Valley Road, approximately 8–10 minutes west of I-17 via Carefree Highway or Dove Valley Road. All drive-time estimates in this guide measure from those two access points, not from a downtown Phoenix reference.

Neighborhoods — Profiled for TSMC Employees

Eight communities — in five distinct market tiers — serve TSMC employees. The right choice depends on whether you want a short commute with master-planned amenities, strong school districts for your children, desert acreage for privacy, or high-desert resort living. Here is an honest breakdown of each.

Closest to Campus
Norterra & Sonoran Foothills
Zip: 85085  ·  To TSMC: 10–18 min  ·  District: DVUSD (A-rated)

The neighborhood with the most direct commute to the TSMC campus — roughly 8–10 miles via Loop 303 or Dove Valley Road, 10–18 minutes in typical morning traffic. Norterra is a master-planned cluster of communities including Fireside at Norterra, Sonoran Foothills, Tramonto, Stetson Hills, Stetson Valley, Dynamite Mountain Ranch, Union Park, and Sonoran Gate. Homes range from townhomes in the high $300Ks to single-family homes at $500K–$800K+. Fireside at Norterra has a 16,000 sq ft community center with a heated lap pool, sauna, hot tub, fitness center, rock climbing wall, tennis, and basketball — a genuine resort amenity package. Direct trail access to the 10,000-acre Phoenix Sonoran Preserve. The Shops at Norterra (Harkins Theatre, The Sicilian Butcher, gym, groceries) is a 10-minute drive. USAA, Honeywell, and other major North Phoenix employers are also within this corridor.

Semi-Rural Acreage
Desert Hills
Zip: 85086  ·  To TSMC: 20–30 min  ·  District: DVUSD or CCUSD — verify per parcel

Desert Hills sits in the middle of the 85086 zip code between Anthem to the south and New River to the north, east of I-17. It is not a master-planned community — it is a semi-rural corridor of 1 to 5-acre custom and semi-custom homes with no master HOA, no community fees, and considerable privacy. Dominion Group Properties specializes in Desert Hills — the brokerage is on Carefree Highway, the primary commercial corridor for this area, and handles land transactions, custom builds, and resale. The right Desert Hills address gives an employee a custom home on acreage with a 20–25 minute straight shot down I-17 to the TSMC campus entrance at 43rd Avenue. School district varies by parcel — some addresses are DVUSD, some are Cave Creek Unified. Verify with both districts before committing. Typical prices: $650K–$1.8M.

Master-Planned Family
Anthem
Zip: 85086 / 85087  ·  To TSMC: 25–35 min  ·  District: DVUSD (A-rated, all 5 high schools)

Anthem is a Del Webb master-planned community of approximately 6,000 homes in two distinct sub-markets. Anthem Parkside (85086) is the larger non-gated side — townhomes from $360K, single-family from $500K–$900K. Anthem Country Club (85086/85087) is a gated golf community with single-story estate homes from $750K to $2M+. Anthem Community Park is the lifestyle anchor: 63 acres with a full community center, water park, skate park, tennis, basketball, and an event calendar. All five DVUSD high schools received Arizona ADE "A" grades for 2024–25. Boulder Creek High School, which serves Anthem, is an A-rated school that in 2025 was named an Arizona A+ School of Excellence. Anthem Country Club includes 18 holes of golf and a clubhouse. Listed median in Anthem as of June 2026: approximately $604K; typical resale range $520K–$900K depending on sub-community.

Rural Acreage
New River
Zip: 85087  ·  To TSMC: 30–40 min  ·  District: DVUSD (A-rated)

New River is the rural tier — 2 to 5+ acre parcels on private wells and septic, mostly no-HOA, bordering the Tonto National Forest to the north. The lifestyle is genuinely rural: dark skies, low density, horses and RVs are normal. The I-17 is the commute corridor; 30–40 minutes south to TSMC. New River is served by DVUSD with Boulder Creek High School among its feeder high schools. Median sale price rose to $695K in June 2026 (up approximately 7% year-over-year), with active inventory compressing significantly. Buyers who want large lots at a discount to comparable Cave Creek acreage and who don't need the master-planned amenity package will find New River the strongest value in the TSMC commute corridor. Well and septic due diligence required.

Desert Character
Cave Creek
Zip: 85331  ·  To TSMC: 25–35 min  ·  District: CCUSD (B+)

Cave Creek is 25–35 minutes east of TSMC via Carefree Highway to I-17, or via the 101. The 85331 zip spans several distinct markets: Tatum Ranch (3,400+ homes, townhomes from $400Ks, SFR $600K–$900K), Dove Valley Ranch (gated golf community, Robert Trent Jones Jr. public course), and the Cave Creek foothills custom corridor (2–5 acres, no HOA, $1M–$3M+). Cave Creek Unified School District (B+, Niche 2026, ranked top 30 in Arizona) serves all of 85331. Cactus Shadows High School serves the Cave Creek community. The Old Town Cave Creek character — western dining, trail access, dark skies — attracts employees who want personality over polish. Tatum Ranch delivers the amenity package without the HOA-intensity of Anthem. Dominion Group Properties has specific expertise in Cave Creek through its Carefree Highway office.

Luxury Resort
Carefree
Zip: 85377  ·  To TSMC: 30–40 min  ·  District: CCUSD (B+)

Carefree is the high-desert resort tier — 3,800 residents on 8.8 square miles, median sale price $1.31M (June 2026). New Construction
North Gateway & Halo Vista
Zip: 85085  ·  To TSMC: 10–20 min  ·  District: DVUSD

The emerging new-construction corridor directly south of the TSMC campus along 51st Avenue and the Loop 303. Halo Vista — the master development announced in late 2024 from TSMC's partnership with Pulte Group — will eventually encompass thousands of new homes, retail, and hotel space on approximately 6,000 acres of state trust land. Early-phase production home communities (Lennar's Middle Vistas and others) are active now, with 10–20 minute commutes to the fab entrance. For employees who prefer brand-new construction with energy efficiency and modern layouts, this corridor is the practical choice. Builder incentives on rate buy-downs and closing costs are active as of mid-2026. The trade-off: limited established retail and services compared to the Norterra corridor 10 minutes south.

Best for dual-commute households willing to trade a longer TSMC drive for proximity to North Scottsdale amenities.

Public School Districts

Two districts serve the TSMC commute corridor. For families with school-age children, the school district question often drives the neighborhood decision more than the commute. Here is an honest comparison.

Deer Valley Unified School District (DVUSD)

DVUSD is an Arizona ADE "A" district — 93% of its 42 schools received a "B" or higher grade for 2024–25, and all five of its comprehensive high schools received "A" grades. The district serves approximately 32,800 students across North Phoenix, from the Norterra corridor north through Anthem and New River. Key facts: 17:1 student-teacher ratio; 97.9% licensed teachers; dual enrollment and AP programs at all high schools; the Canyon Springs STEM Academy is a Cognia-certified STEM school serving K–8 with hands-on inquiry-based learning in science, technology, engineering, and math. DVUSD also runs open enrollment, meaning families can apply to specific schools of interest beyond their boundary school.

Cave Creek Unified School District (CCUSD)

CCUSD serves all of Cave Creek (85331) and Carefree (85377), rated B+ by Niche in 2026 and ranked in Arizona's top 30 districts. The district is smaller and more community-oriented than DVUSD — approximately 12,000 students across 10 schools. All feeder schools for Cave Creek and Carefree lead to Cactus Shadows High School, which offers AP coursework, athletics, and a tight-knit community culture. CCUSD is the right district for families who prioritize a smaller district with a strong community identity over the comprehensive scale of DVUSD.

CCUSD key schools (Cave Creek & Carefree addresses): Black Mountain Elementary → Sonoran Trails Middle School → Cactus Shadows High School. Confirm specific feeder school for any address with CCUSD directly before purchasing.

Paradise Valley Unified School District (PVSchools)

Serves the Desert Ridge and north Scottsdale corridor. ADE "A" district with highly regarded academic programming including International Baccalaureate, Mandarin immersion, Spanish immersion, STEAM, and Gifted services at multiple campuses. For Taiwanese families who want Mandarin immersion in a public school setting, PVSchools is the most practical option in the metro area. This is the school district that pushes some dual-commute families toward Desert Ridge despite its longer TSMC drive.

Mandarin immersion in public schools: PVSchools offers a Mandarin Chinese immersion program at specific elementary campuses. This is the only Mandarin immersion public school option in the immediate TSMC commute corridor. Enrollment is open but competitive — contact PVSchools at pvschools.net for current availability and campus locations.

Private Schools — Options for TSMC Families

Many TSMC families — particularly those relocating from Taiwan and other Asian markets — prioritize private school options for academic rigor, international community, or language programs. Phoenix's private school market is larger than most relocating employees expect. Here are the most relevant options organized by what they offer.

International & Language Immersion Programs

  • International School of Arizona (ISA) 9522 E San Salvador Dr, Scottsdale, AZ 85258  ·  (480) 874-2326  ·  isaz.org Grades: 18 months through 8th grade. French and Spanish immersion programs accredited by France and Spain. Voted Best Private School in Phoenix 2023 and 2024. For families from Taiwan, the emphasis on multilingual education and international curriculum has made ISA a frequent choice. Drive from TSMC-area neighborhoods: 30–40 minutes.
  • Amici Trilingual Montessori Phoenix area  ·  English, Spanish, and Mandarin trilingual immersion Grades: Infant through elementary. Every classroom has a trilingual teacher in English, Spanish, and Mandarin. One of the very few programs in Phoenix offering Mandarin immersion from infancy. Particularly relevant for Taiwanese families wanting to maintain Mandarin for children born in the US. Contact for current locations and availability.

STEM & Academic Rigor Focus

  • Great Hearts Academies (multiple campuses) Multiple North Phoenix and Scottsdale campuses  ·  greatheartsamerica.org Grades K–12. Classical liberal arts curriculum — literature, Latin, Socratic discussion, logic, philosophy, and mathematics. Not a STEM-focused curriculum in the traditional sense, but consistently produces students with the analytical and reasoning foundations relevant to engineering families who want rigorous academics over rote learning. Open enrollment charter schools, no tuition. Waitlists are common; apply early.
  • Desert Garden Montessori 5130 E Warner Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85044  ·  (480) 496-9833  ·  desertgardenmontessori.org Grades: Infant through 12th grade. The only private school in Arizona with an International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme. Also Cognia certified. Full range of enrichment programs including art, music, theater, service trips, and a community service emphasis. The IB designation is meaningful for families who expect to relocate again internationally — IB credentials transfer globally.
  • North Valley Christian Academy 33655 N 27th Dr, Phoenix, AZ 85085  ·  (623) 551-3454  ·  northvalleyca.org Grades K–12. Located directly in the 85085 corridor — one of the few private K–12 options with a 15–20 minute drive from Norterra or Sonoran Foothills. Christian faith-based. For families of faith who want a private school with minimal commute from the TSMC-proximate neighborhoods, North Valley Christian is the most convenient option.
  • Notre Dame Preparatory (NDP) 9701 E Bell Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85260  ·  (480) 634-8200  ·  ndpsaints.org Grades 9–12. Catholic college preparatory high school. One of the most rigorous private high school options in the Phoenix area. AP programs across all disciplines. Strong college placement record. Drive from Norterra: approximately 30 minutes. More relevant for families in Cave Creek, Carefree, or Desert Ridge where the commute is manageable.
Practical note on private school waitlists: Phoenix's top private schools — particularly Great Hearts, ISA, and Desert Garden Montessori — maintain waitlists that can run 6–18 months for popular grade levels. If private school is part of your housing decision, contact schools immediately upon accepting your TSMC offer and do not wait until relocation is complete. Proximity to the school should factor into your neighborhood selection.

Why Cave Creek Attracts TSMC Employees

Cave Creek offers something the Loop 303 corridor master-planned communities cannot: genuine desert character with lot sizes that make breathing room feel permanent rather than aspirational. The 85331 zip code spans Tatum Ranch — 3,400+ homes with townhomes from $400K and family homes to $900K — through the Cave Creek foothills where custom homes on 2 to 5 acres start around $1M. Cave Creek Unified School District serves all of 85331 with a B+ Niche rating and consistently ranked schools. Cactus Shadows High School is the feeder for all Cave Creek families. The Old Town commercial district on Cave Creek Road has a working cluster of restaurants, bars, and shops that give the community a social gravity the Loop 303 new-construction corridor has not yet developed. The 2,922-acre Cave Creek Regional Park and 2,154-acre Spur Cross Ranch Conservation Area — both accessible in under 10 minutes from Cave Creek — provide serious hiking, mountain biking, and trail running infrastructure that outdoor-oriented employees from Taiwan (accustomed to Taiwan's extensive trail systems) find immediately compelling.

Why Carefree Attracts Senior TSMC Employees

Senior engineers, managers, and executives who have spent careers in Taiwan's technology industry — in Hsinchu's science park environment — often look for a residential quality that goes beyond the standard American suburban master-plan. Carefree delivers that. The Boulders Resort and Spa (Condé Nast Traveler-recognized, Jay Morrish golf courses) is the resort infrastructure. The Carefree Town Center on Easy Street provides a walkable commercial cluster with fine art galleries, quality restaurants, and a Friday Farmers Market year-round. The lack of a local real estate tax means property taxes run approximately half those of comparable Scottsdale properties — meaningful at the $1.5M–$3M price tier where many senior TSMC employees will be shopping. Carefree is also where the SkyRanch fly-in community operates — Arizona's first fly-in residential community, relevant for engineers who hold pilot certificates. The 30–38 minute commute from Carefree to TSMC is the trade-off — sustainable, but longer than Norterra.
