TSMC Arizona Salary Data — What the DOL Filings Show

TSMC Arizona Corporation files Labor Condition Applications (LCAs) with the U.S. Department of Labor before sponsoring H-1B employees. These filings are public record and represent the closest approximation to actual base salaries — they are the minimum wage TSMC must pay for each role, and in practice tend to closely match actual offers.

Financing on an H-1B or TN Visa

Most TSMC employees on H-1B visas can obtain conventional mortgage financing in the U.S. — but the requirements are specific and vary by lender. Dominion's in-house lending through Foothills Mortgage Group LLC (NMLS 1904177) has experience with non-citizen and visa-holder transactions. Key points:

  • H-1B holders are eligible for conventional (Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac), FHA, and many portfolio loan products. A valid H-1B visa with at least 12 months remaining, two years of U.S. employment history (or a signed employment contract for new hires), and a U.S. credit profile are the standard requirements.
  • No SSN yet? An Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) can be used for initial banking and credit establishment. SSN typically arrives 2–4 weeks after arrival. Some lenders will pre-approve with an ITIN pending SSN issuance.
  • Credit history is the most common obstacle for newly arrived employees. U.S. lenders cannot use Taiwan credit history. Start building U.S. credit immediately upon arrival: secured credit card, auto loan, or credit-builder loan. 12 months of U.S. credit history significantly expands your options.
  • Down payment: Foreign funds wired from Taiwan for down payment must be properly documented — a gift letter is not sufficient for wired international funds. Your lender will need 60 days of account statements showing the funds were in a U.S. account, or full source-of-funds documentation for recent wire transfers.
