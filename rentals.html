Why Most TSMC Employees Rent First

Buying immediately upon arrival is possible but rarely advisable. The North Phoenix market has real sub-neighborhood variation that takes time to understand — the commute from a specific address to the fab entrance, the school that actually serves that parcel, the HOA layer count in a gated community, the wildfire insurance constraints on a foothills lot. Employees who rent for 6–18 months make better purchase decisions than those who buy in week two. The rental period also builds the U.S. credit history that expands your mortgage options when you are ready to buy.

Rent vs. Buy — How to Think About It

For TSMC employees on H-1B visas in their first 12 months in Phoenix, renting while building a U.S. credit profile is almost always the right financial decision. Here is a practical timeline:

  • Month 1–3: Arrive, rent, get your Arizona driver's license (MVD appointment at azmvdnow.gov — walk-ins wait 2+ hours; appointment gets you out in 30 minutes). Open a U.S. bank account, apply for a secured credit card, start building U.S. credit history. Research neighborhoods during your commute.
  • Month 4–6: You now have 3–6 months of U.S. credit history. Begin conversations with Dominion about pre-qualification. Your employer letter, two recent pay stubs, and bank statements are the core documents. If your SSN is established and your H-1B has 12+ months remaining, most programs are accessible.
  • Month 6–12: With 6–12 months of U.S. credit and employment history, your mortgage options are significantly broader. This is the sweet spot for most TSMC employees to begin seriously touring homes and writing offers.
  • Month 12–18: Most TSMC employees who arrived in Phoenix close on a home within 12–18 months of arrival, based on the community pattern observed since 2022.
Build credit from day one: A secured credit card (deposit-backed, available to H-1B holders with no U.S. credit history) used responsibly for 12 months creates the credit foundation that expands your mortgage options. Capital One, Discover, and several local credit unions offer secured cards with no SSN requirement — only an ITIN. Pay the full balance monthly.
