For TSMC Families Considering Private Education

Private Schools Near TSMC Phoenix

Mandarin immersion from infancy, IB programs, STEM academics, and classical education — the six private school options most relevant to TSMC families, with addresses, tuition notes, and waitlist realities.

Home › Private Schools
Waitlist reality: Phoenix's top private schools — Great Hearts, ISA, Desert Garden Montessori — maintain waitlists of 6–18 months for popular grade levels. Contact schools the day you accept your TSMC offer. Do not wait until relocation is complete. Proximity to the school should influence which neighborhood you choose.

Mandarin Immersion — Priority for Taiwanese Families

  • Amici Trilingual Montessori Phoenix metro area  ·  English, Spanish, and Mandarin trilingual immersion Grades: Infant through elementary. Every classroom has a trilingual teacher in English, Spanish, and Mandarin. One of the only programs in Phoenix offering Mandarin immersion from infancy — particularly relevant for Taiwanese families wanting to maintain Mandarin for children born in the U.S. or who arrive as young children. Contact for current campus locations and enrollment availability.

International & Multilingual Programs

  • International School of Arizona (ISA) 9522 E San Salvador Dr, Scottsdale, AZ 85258  ·  (480) 874-2326  ·  isaz.net Grades: 18 months through 8th grade. French and Spanish immersion programs accredited by France and Spain. Voted Best Private School in Phoenix 2023 and 2024. For families from Taiwan, the multilingual international curriculum and non-American academic framing has made ISA a consistent choice among TSMC expat families. Drive from Norterra: 30–40 minutes. Drive from Cave Creek/Carefree: 25–35 minutes.

International Baccalaureate (IB)

  • Desert Garden Montessori 5130 E Warner Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85044  ·  (480) 496-9833  ·  desertgardenmontessori.org Grades: Infant through 12th grade. The only private school in Arizona with an International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (IB MYP). Also Cognia certified. Full enrichment program: art, music, theater, service trips. IB credentials transfer globally — directly relevant for TSMC families who expect to relocate again internationally. Tuition varies by grade level; contact for current rates.

Classical Academic & STEM Options

  • Great Hearts Academies (multiple campuses) Multiple North Phoenix and Scottsdale campuses  ·  greatheartsamerica.org Grades K–12. Classical liberal arts curriculum — literature, Latin, Socratic discussion, logic, philosophy, and mathematics. Open enrollment charter schools — free tuition. Consistently produces students with strong analytical foundations. For TSMC families who want rigorous academics and are comfortable with the classical (rather than STEM-specific) curriculum emphasis. Waitlists are common; apply immediately. The Veritas campus in Scottsdale and Anthem Preparatory in Anthem are the most relevant for TSMC employees.
  • North Valley Christian Academy (NVCA) 33655 N 27th Dr, Phoenix, AZ 85085  ·  (623) 551-3454  ·  northvalleyca.org Grades K–12. Located directly in the 85085 corridor — one of the only private K–12 options within 15–20 minutes of Norterra or Sonoran Foothills. Christian faith-based. For TSMC families of faith who want private school with minimal additional commute from TSMC-proximate neighborhoods, NVCA is the most convenient option.

Private High School

  • Notre Dame Preparatory (NDP) 9701 E Bell Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85260  ·  (480) 634-8200  ·  ndpsaints.org Grades 9–12. Catholic college preparatory high school. One of the most academically rigorous private high schools in the Phoenix area. AP programs across all disciplines. Strong college placement record (top universities including UC system, Ivies, and major engineering programs). Drive from Norterra: approximately 30 minutes. More relevant for families in Cave Creek, Carefree, or Desert Ridge where the commute is manageable.
